# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

Michael Siddi in Italy is responsible for the personal data described here. For privacy questions or requests, email [hello@michaelsiddi.com](mailto:hello@michaelsiddi.com).

The app has no ads and no ad tracking. Your collection is stored on your device by default, not on our servers. You can back it up to your own iCloud account, managed by Apple, or export it as a ZIP file. We cannot access those copies unless you choose to share them.

Camera, photos, microphone and location are used only for the features that ask for them. Timegrapher audio is processed on your device and is not sent to us. You can choose to share a diagnostic recording. Apple handles location and address lookups.

The app connects to public time servers, Lunette's API for catalog updates, news, templates and exchange rates and listing sites you add to your Wishlist. These services receive technical connection data such as your IP address. Our website host and Google Fonts can receive similar data. The app never sends your collection to us.

Usage statistics help us improve Lunette. Events record a fixed action name, a UTC timestamp and an order number, for example opening news, saving a watch, using the audio timegrapher or completing a Pro purchase. Each session has a randomly generated code and a start time. Requests also identify the app version, operating system version and distribution environment such as TestFlight. They contain no account or device identifier, collection contents, watch identifiers, article or listing URLs, search text, photos, documents, journal entries, audio, measurements, receipts or transaction identifiers.

A fresh app launch creates a new session. Returning after less than five minutes in the background continues the same session and records a resume event. Returning after five minutes or more creates a new session. We do not use the session code to identify you or connect your activity across sessions.

When a session starts, the app attempts one immediate request to show session starts in real time. Completed sessions are temporarily queued on your device and sent to our API at a later startup or foreground return. A queued file is deleted after acknowledgment, or on the next cleanup if it is more than seven days old. Usage files are excluded from collection exports and backups. The server turns events into aggregate statistics rather than retaining a permanent activity history. Minimal records used to prevent duplicate counting may remain for up to 14 days.

These requests go to Lunette's API, whose address is configured by lunetteapp.com/api/v1/config.json. Network infrastructure receives technical connection information such as your IP address. We do not use it to identify you, fingerprint your device or connect sessions. Usage statistics are not used for advertising or shared with advertising networks. We process them based on our legitimate interest in improving the app.

You can stop this collection at any time using “Opt out of usage tracking” at the very bottom of Settings. Turning it on stops new usage events and requests and deletes queued usage files. It cannot recall requests already delivered. The choice is saved in the app database and included in your normal backups, so restoring a backup restores that choice too. Turning tracking back on creates a new session code.

If you use our [contact form](https://lunetteapp.com/contact), FormSubmit.co delivers your name, email, message and any device details to us by email. We use this information to answer you, based on our legitimate interest in handling requests. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or to restrict or object to its use. You can also complain to the [Italian Data Protection Authority](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/). Deleting the app removes its local data. Delete any iCloud backup through your Apple account.
