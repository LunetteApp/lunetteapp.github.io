# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

## Overview

Lunette is a watch collection management app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how Lunette handles your data.

## Data Collection

The app does not require an account and does not use analytics or ad tracking. Your collection is stored on your device. If you enable iCloud backup, a copy goes to your Apple account. Network requests may reveal your IP address to the destination. If you contact us, we receive the information you submit through the contact form.

## Network Connections

Lunette uses limited network connections:

- **Time references:** Public time servers provide a reference for accuracy checks.
- **App content:** Lunette's API provides watch catalog updates, news and story templates. News images may load from publisher websites.
- **Wishlist listings:** When you add or refresh a listing URL, the app visits that site to retrieve price and image information. The site may receive your IP address. If you open a listing or news article, the destination may use its own cookies and privacy policy.
- **Optional iCloud backup:** Apple handles backups in your own iCloud account when you enable this feature.

The app does not send your watch collection to Lunette's API.

## Data Storage

Your watch collection, journal, photos, optional location tags, service records, Wishlist and profile are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud backup, the backup may include these data in your Apple account. If you export or share content, it goes to the destination you choose.

## iCloud Backup

If you choose to enable iCloud backup within the app, your data is synced through your personal Apple iCloud account. This data:

- Is encrypted and managed by Apple according to their [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/)
- Is accessible only through your Apple ID
- Is not accessible to us in any way

You can disable iCloud backup at any time within the app settings.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising. Its network connections are described above. The website loads fonts from Google and a Product Hunt badge, so those providers may receive your IP address when you visit the site. The contact form is processed by FormSubmit.co as described below.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your collection data. We cannot view your local collection or your personal iCloud backup. Apple receives backup data only if you enable iCloud backup. External sites receive requests when you open their articles or listings. FormSubmit.co receives the information you send through the contact form.

## Data Deletion

You can delete individual records in the app. Deleting the app removes local app data from that device. If you enabled iCloud backup, delete that backup separately through your Apple account. To ask about deletion of correspondence sent through the contact form, contact us using the details below.

## Children's Privacy

Lunette does not knowingly collect information from children. The app contains no objectionable content and is rated 4+.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date.

## Contact Form

When you use the contact form on our website or through the app, we collect:

- **Name**, to address you in our reply
- **Email address**, to respond to your inquiry
- **Message content**, to understand your request
- **Device information** (if sent from the app), app version, iOS version, device model and Pro status, to help diagnose issues

This data is processed through [FormSubmit.co](https://formsubmit.co), a third-party form handling service, and delivered to us via email. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not use it for marketing or share it with any other third party. We retain correspondence only as long as needed to resolve your request.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through our [contact form](https://lunetteapp.com/contact).
