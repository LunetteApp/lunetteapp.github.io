How it's built.

Offline-first

Your collection and journal work offline. You can choose to back up your data to your own iCloud account.

Zero tracking

No analytics or ad tracking in the app. Your collection stays on your device unless you choose iCloud backup or export.

One-time purchase

Add up to five watches for free. Lunette Pro raises limits and unlocks extras. Timegrapher Pro is sold separately. Both are one-time purchases.

Watch of the Day

Pick what you wore and make a shareable story with included gradients. Extra backgrounds are available with Lunette Pro.

Service history

Keep service dates, costs and watchmaker contacts with each watch.

Crafted palettes
